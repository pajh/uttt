Sub-board score

Edit one local 3×3 board and inspect the X sub-board score: the number of future X moves still needed to finish a live line, less the protection credit and one twentieth for every improving move. Lower is better, this is a distance, not a normalized higher-is-better score. Every calculation runs in integer twentieths and is converted for display only.

Local position editor

Click a cell to cycle Empty → X → O → Empty. Editing is allowed even after a line is completed; this is not alternating-turn play.
